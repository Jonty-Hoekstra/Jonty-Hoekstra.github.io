EARLY CAREER · JELLYFISH & GIANTS

Mythos Party

From early in my career, on a title called Mythos Party. I was the designer on the project and created three maps for it; two of them are shown here. Each map went from paper design to the in-engine build, including some set dressing. I also designed the core combat loop and abilities, and implemented the multiplayer abilities in C++.
