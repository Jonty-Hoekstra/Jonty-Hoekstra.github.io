About

● Remote · New Zealand

Hello, I'm Jonty

I'm a senior game designer with over five years of industry experience, leading projects and communicating across disciplines. I mix collaboration with independent iteration, and I bring both creative and technical thinking to a problem.

Much of my work is level design, from paper design and blockout through to set dressing, in Unreal Engine. Early in my career I also put a lot of effort into tech art and VFX, so I enjoy building the materials and effects that bring a level to life.
